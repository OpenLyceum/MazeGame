# Code review — 2026-10-04

## Findings addressed

- **P1 — arena boundary ignored by collision detection:** `tileAt` returned WALL outside the grid, but circle collision skipped out-of-bounds tiles. Circle collision now recognizes all four arena edges and points outside the grid, allowing velocity-mode push-back to stop escape.
- **P2 — timer pauses on returning to start:** elapsed time only advanced while displaced from the start. A per-run latch now keeps it running after the first movement and clears on retry/reset.
